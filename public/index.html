What this desk checks before it hands you an address

NEAR Intents 1Click does the routing. This desk treats every answer from it, and from every price feed and node, as untrusted until checked.

  • Your addresses are real and spendable. Checksums on every chain (EIP-55, bech32/bech32m, base58check). Burn addresses, token contracts, Solana token accounts and programs, missing NEAR accounts, and anyone-can-spend Bitcoin outputs are refused.
  • The router quoted what you asked for. Its answer must echo your assets, amount, recipient and refund address exactly, carry no memo, and keep the deposit address alive long enough to pay.
  • The price is fair. Cost is measured against an independent market price (CoinGecko, DeFiLlama as fallback), never the router's own. An outlier in either direction is blocked.
  • The deal you accepted is the deal you get. A real quote more than 0.25% worse than the preview needs a fresh yes.
  • One press, one swap. Each request is reserved in the ledger before the router is called, so a double tap, a retry or a dropped connection returns the same swap, never a second address.
  • "Delivered" is checked, not believed. A payout is confirmed on the destination chain (EVM receipts and transfer logs, Solana balances, Bitcoin outputs, NEAR payout receipts), must be newer than the swap, and can settle one swap only. A contradiction pauses the route automatically.
  • Paying from a connected wallet sends exactly the card. On the 8 EVM chains your browser wallet can send the deposit for you: one plain transfer of the exact amount, to the deposit address, on the swap's chain, built from what was fixed when the address was issued. Never an approval or a signature. The wallet is switched to the right chain first, and once a swap is paid this way, that browser does not offer to pay it again.
  • You get a proof you can check. Every settled swap has a signed proof of delivery: what was checked, for whom, how much, and the payout transaction. Anyone can check the signature, and the explorer link shows the payout without trusting this desk.
How it works: you get a one-time deposit address from the NEAR Intents 1Click service. Send the exact amount from any wallet, or let a connected browser wallet send it on the EVM chains; market makers fill the swap and pay your receiving address. If it cannot be filled at or above the guaranteed minimum, the deposit is refunded to your refund address.
Custody: this desk never holds, signs or moves your funds and never sees your keys. It has no wallet and no treasury. When you pay from a connected wallet, your wallet signs, after you approve it there.
Costs: every quote shows its full cost against the market price, including 1Click's fee. This desk adds no fee unless shown.
Support: support@cryptolwa.io. Include your swap id and receipt. We never ask for your keys, seed phrase or a payment.
